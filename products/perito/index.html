Perito for Jira In development

Provided by Axirria Digital Vietnam Limited Company

Perito writes test cases for a Jira issue using the issues around it as context. It asks your team about unclear requirements before guessing, suggests tests for areas a change may affect, and warns you about tests you may already have.
