Perito for Jira – Data Processing Addendum

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This Addendum applies when Axirria Digital Vietnam Limited Company ("Processor") processes personal data on behalf of a customer ("Controller") through Perito for Jira.

  1. Subject and duration: processing of Jira data needed to generate test cases, for as long as Perito is installed.
  2. Categories of data: Atlassian account IDs; names or other personal data that users include in Jira issues.
  3. Processor obligations: process data only on the Controller's documented instructions (installing and using Perito); keep data confidential; apply appropriate technical and organisational security measures; assist the Controller with data subject requests; notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach.
  4. Sub-processors: Atlassian (hosting and storage on the Forge platform); OpenAI (processing of issue text to generate test cases). We will update this page before adding a new sub-processor.
  5. International transfers: issue text sent for test generation is processed by OpenAI, which may process it in the United States. Data is sent over encrypted connections. OpenAI does not use API data to train its models and may keep it in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days, unless longer retention is required by law.
  6. Deletion: on uninstall, data stored by Perito is deleted as described in the Privacy Policy.
  7. Contact: contact@axirriadigital.com
